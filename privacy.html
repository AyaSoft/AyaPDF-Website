Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Overview

AyaPDF is designed to generate PDF documents directly within your browser. Protecting your privacy is one of our core principles. Most processing is performed locally on your device, and AyaPDF does not collect the content of your ChatGPT conversations or exported PDF documents.

Information We Collect

AyaPDF collects a limited amount of anonymous usage information to help improve the extension and understand which features are used most frequently.

This information may include:

  • Anonymous installation identifier
  • Extension version
  • Browser type
  • Operating system
  • Device language
  • Anonymous feature usage (such as printing a conversation, printing a response, opening documentation, or checking for updates)

This information does not identify you personally.

Information We Do Not Collect

AyaPDF does not collect, transmit, store, or analyze:

  • ChatGPT conversations
  • Prompts or responses
  • Exported PDF documents
  • Images contained in conversations
  • Account credentials
  • Personally identifiable information

Local Processing

PDF generation and formatting are performed locally inside your browser. Your conversation content remains on your device and is never transmitted to AyaSoft servers.

Analytics

AyaPDF uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymous usage statistics. These analytics help us improve the extension, identify compatibility issues, and understand which features are most valuable to users.

Google Analytics does not receive your ChatGPT conversations, prompts, responses, or exported PDF content.

Third-Party Services

AyaPDF uses Google Analytics 4 to process anonymous usage analytics. No personal information, ChatGPT conversations, or exported PDF content is shared with Google or any other third party.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page. Continued use of AyaPDF after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@ayasoft.es .
