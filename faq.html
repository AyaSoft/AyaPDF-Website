Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about AyaPDF.

Is AyaPDF free?

Yes. AyaPDF is free to download and use.

Which browsers are supported?

AyaPDF supports Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

Can I export code with syntax highlighting?

Yes. AyaPDF preserves syntax highlighting for supported programming languages.

Can images be included in the PDF?

Yes. Images displayed in the conversation are exported with the document.

Can I customize page size and margins?

Yes. You can configure paper size, orientation, margins, headers, footers and many other options.

Does AyaPDF require an internet connection?

Only ChatGPT requires internet access. PDF generation itself runs locally in your browser.
